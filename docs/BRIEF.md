# The brief (FutureProofing take-home)

> Saved verbatim in its essentials so every decision in this repo can be traced back to it.

**Task.** Prototype a website for a fictional children's cancer foundation (invent the name and identity).

**The foundation's goals.** Support families through treatment, raise funds, recruit volunteers.

**Scope.** Don't build the whole site. Pick the slice where AI genuinely makes the product better and go deep. Everything else can be reasonable placeholders.

**What they evaluate.**

1. **Judgment** — what was built, what was left out, and why.
2. **Quality of the AI integration** — grounding, failure modes, guardrails, cost awareness, and whether the experience makes sense for a stressed parent at 2 a.m.
3. **How you work** — the AI-native workflow: tools, prompts, what the agent did vs. what the human kept.
4. **Code and product sense** — structure, readability, small details.
5. **Tone** — sensitive domain: no fake testimonials with names, no invented medical claims, no stock sadness.

**Deliverables.**

- Deployed URL.
- Repository with a README covering decisions, what's next with more time, and the process with AI.
- Optional: a 3–5 minute screen recording.
