Lumi is a foundation for families of children with cancer. We focus on the practical side of treatment: hospital days, money and work, school and family, and looking after yourself. Lumi doesn't give medical advice. Your child's care team does that.

Note: Lumi is a fictional foundation in a prototype website. The services below marked "placeholder" don't exist yet.

## Ask Lumi

The "Ask Lumi" page answers practical questions in English or Spanish, any time of day.

- Lumi uses the guides in this library to write answers and includes links to them. AI answers can contain mistakes; a linked guide may not support every statement.
- If Lumi doesn't have a guide on something, it says so and suggests who to ask.
- For anything about your child's health, Lumi will point you to your care team.
- Lumi doesn't save your conversation. To find an answer, your question is sent to OpenAI, the company that runs the AI model. Please don't include names or details that identify your family.
- If Lumi can't answer, you can choose to send your question to the Lumi team so they can write a new guide. You'll see exactly what is saved and can edit it first. The team can read it, and questions older than 30 days are deleted when someone saves a question or staff opens the list.

## What Lumi is not

- Not a medical service, and not a replacement for your care team.
- Not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911. Outside the US, contact your local emergency services.
- Not a crisis line. If you're in emotional crisis, see "Taking care of yourself as a parent", which has the number to call or text.

## Who to ask

For your child's health: the care team. For practical help at the hospital: the hospital social worker. For questions about Lumi: the family team, once it exists.
